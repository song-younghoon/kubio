Kubio kubio.json v0.10 Proposal
Status: Deferred features; discussion not started
Base: v0.9


1. Purpose

This proposal carries every candidate deferred by v0.9. Sections 2 through 8
are the exhaustive carried set. They define no agreed behavior yet, and the
scope must be narrowed before promotion.


2. Additional route matching

- Client-IP conditions, including forwarded client-IP derivation.
- Presence or absence conditions.
- Negation, regular expressions, wildcard values, and numeric comparisons.
- Header or query value capture and route-scoped logical OR groups.


3. Backend resilience and upstream transport

- Active and passive health checks.
- Weighted target selection.
- Status-based retries.
- Request-body replay and explicit idempotency policies.
- Retry backoff, total deadlines, and retry budgets.
- Direct-target timeout options and response-body timeouts.
- HTTPS upstream CA and SNI settings.


4. Client-facing TLS

- Multiple manual certificates and certificate selection by SNI or host.
- HTTP-to-HTTPS redirects and automatic ACME certificates.
- Mutual TLS, TLS passthrough, and configurable TLS protocol or algorithm
  policy.


5. Observability and administration

- Configurable access-log outputs and formats.
- Custom fields, sampling, and redaction rules.
- Query-string and request/response-header logging.
- Request IDs and forwarded client-IP fields.
- Per-attempt logs and upstream timing fields.
- Request and upstream metrics.
- Health and metrics endpoints.
- General administration endpoints.
- Reload status reporting.


6. Proxy protocols and routing actions

- RFC Forwarded headers.
- PROXY protocol.
- URI rewriting.
- Redirect responses.
- Fixed responses.
- Custom error bodies.


7. Application gateway features

These remain candidates only when backed by a concrete use case:

- Rate limiting.
- Authentication.
- Response compression.
- Response caching.
- Service discovery.


8. Header policy features

- Response trailer modification.
- Conditional response-header policies.
- Request-header removal and addition.
- Response-header policies for Kubio-generated or intermediate responses.
