Kubio kubio.json v0.16 Proposal
Status: Deferred features; discussion not started
Base: v0.15


1. Purpose

This proposal carries the candidates deferred by v0.15. It is not an agreed
specification yet and must be narrowed before promotion.


2. Backend resilience and upstream transport

- Active and passive health checks and target ejection.
- Adaptive backoff.
- Direct-target connection, response-header, and response-body timeout
  options.
- HTTPS upstream CA and SNI settings.


3. Additional route matching

- Client-IP conditions, including forwarded client-IP derivation.
- Presence or absence conditions, negation, regular expressions, wildcards,
  numeric comparisons, value capture, and logical OR groups.


4. Client-facing TLS

- Multiple manual certificates and SNI/host certificate selection.
- HTTP-to-HTTPS redirects, automatic ACME, mutual TLS, TLS passthrough, and
  configurable TLS protocol or algorithm policy.


5. Observability, administration, and routing actions

- Configurable access logs, custom fields, sampling, redaction, request IDs,
  metrics, health/metrics/admin endpoints, and reload status.
- RFC Forwarded and PROXY protocols.
- URI rewriting, redirects, fixed responses, and custom error bodies.


6. Application gateway and header policies

- Rate limiting, authentication, compression, caching, and service discovery.
- Response trailers, conditional response headers, request-header removal or
  addition, and policies for Kubio-generated or intermediate responses.
