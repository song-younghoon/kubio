Kubio kubio.json v0.11 Proposal
Status: Deferred features; discussion not started
Base: v0.10


1. Purpose

This proposal carries the v0.10 candidates that are intentionally outside the
small status-retry scope. No behavior below is agreed yet. The list is
exhaustive for the deferred v0.10 material and must be narrowed before any
promotion.


2. Backend resilience and upstream transport

- Request-body replay and explicit idempotency policies.
- Retry backoff, total deadlines, and retry budgets.
- Active and passive health checks.
- Weighted target selection.
- Direct-target timeout options, including connection and response-header
  timeouts, and response-body timeouts.
- HTTPS upstream CA and SNI settings.


3. Additional route matching

- Client-IP conditions, including forwarded client-IP derivation.
- Presence or absence conditions.
- Negation, regular expressions, wildcard values, numeric comparisons, value
  capture, and route-scoped logical OR groups.


4. Client-facing TLS

- Multiple manual certificates and certificate selection by SNI or host.
- HTTP-to-HTTPS redirects and automatic ACME certificates.
- Mutual TLS, TLS passthrough, and configurable TLS protocol or algorithm
  policy.


5. Observability and administration

- Configurable access-log outputs and formats, custom fields, sampling, and
  redaction rules.
- Query-string and request/response-header logging.
- Request IDs, forwarded client-IP fields, per-attempt logs, and upstream
  timing fields.
- Request/upstream metrics, health and metrics endpoints, general
  administration endpoints, and reload status reporting.


6. Proxy protocols and routing actions

- RFC Forwarded headers and PROXY protocol.
- URI rewriting, redirect responses, fixed responses, and custom error
  bodies.


7. Application gateway and header policies

- Rate limiting, authentication, response compression, response caching, and
  service discovery.
- Response trailer modification, conditional response-header policies,
  request-header removal/addition, and policies for Kubio-generated or
  intermediate responses.
