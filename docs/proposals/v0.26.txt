Kubio kubio.json v0.26 Proposal
Status: Under review
Base: v0.25


1. Scope under discussion

v0.26 may select one or more capabilities deferred by v0.25. The following
items are candidates only and are not yet part of the agreed specification:

- Custom bodies for Kubio-generated errors.
- Metrics, configurable log sinks and formats, and health, admin, or reload
  endpoints.
- Forwarded and PROXY protocols.
- Listener client authentication, inbound mutual TLS, TLS passthrough,
  configurable TLS policy, ACME, multiple listener certificates, and listener
  SNI certificate selection.
- Authentication and authorization.
- Compression and caching.
- Service discovery.
- Response trailers, conditional response headers, and request-header
  policies.
- Adaptive retry policies.
- Policies for Kubio-generated or intermediate responses.


2. Decision status

The v0.26 scope, configuration names, interaction rules, security behavior,
and compatibility boundaries require discussion before any item is promoted
to docs/specs/v0.26.txt. Items not selected for v0.26 remain candidates for a
later proposal.
